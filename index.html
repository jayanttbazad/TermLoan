What's common across every product

A fixed interest rate for the full tenure, a processing fee capped at 1.5% of the sanctioned amount, and no charge for repaying early after the sixth EMI. There is no prepayment penalty on floating-linked accounts, and part-prepayment is accepted from the seventh month.

What decides your rate

Final pricing depends on your business vintage, GST turnover trend, existing credit obligations, and whether the loan is secured or collateral-free. Applicants with two years of clean repayment history typically land closer to the starting rate.

Eligibility & documents

Most registered MSMEs with at least twelve months of operating history qualify. Check the criteria and required documents below before you apply.

  • ✓

    Business vintage

    At least 12 months of continuous operation under the current ownership.

  • ✓

    Udyam registration

    A valid Udyam Registration Certificate for the applying business entity.

  • ✓

    Minimum turnover

    Annual turnover of ₹6 lakh or more, evidenced by GST returns or bank credits.

  • ✓

    Applicant age

    Proprietor, partners, or directors between 23 and 65 years at loan maturity.

  • ✓

    Credit history

    No default currently reported on existing business or personal credit facilities.

Documents to keep ready

  1. Udyam Registration Certificate

  3. GST registration and last 12 months of returns
  4. Bank statements — last 12 months, primary account
  5. Address proof of business premises
